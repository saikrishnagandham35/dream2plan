SECTION 3: LEGAL REQUIREMENTS (BUDGET-BASED)
================================================================

--- MICRO BUDGET (Below ₹10,000) - BOOTSTRAPPED ---
Recommended Structure: Sole Proprietorship (FREE — zero registration needed)
Required: Nothing mandatory to legally start
Optional:
  - Udyam/MSME Registration (FREE at udyamregistration.gov.in) — recommended
  - Basic bank account (savings works initially)
GST: NOT required until turnover crosses ₹20L (services) or ₹40L (goods)
Company Registration: NOT needed at this stage — wasteful expense
Key Advice: Start → Validate → Generate revenue → Then formalize. Zero cost to begin.

--- SMALL BUDGET (₹10,000 - ₹1,00,000) - LEAN STARTUP ---
Recommended Structure: Sole Proprietorship or Partnership
Registrations:
  - Udyam/MSME Registration (FREE — gives access to all govt schemes)
  - Current bank account
  - DPIIT Startup India Recognition (FREE — takes 2-3 days at startupindia.gov.in)
  - GST only if expecting turnover > ₹20L
Cost Estimate: ₹0 - ₹5,000 total

--- MEDIUM BUDGET (₹1,00,000 - ₹10,00,000) - STANDARD STARTUP ---
Recommended Structure: LLP or Private Limited Company
Steps and Costs:
  1. DSC per director: ₹1,000-2,000
  2. DIN: included in SPICe+ form (MCA portal)
  3. LLP: ₹5,000-10,000 | Pvt Ltd: ₹10,000-20,000 (via CA)
  4. PAN + TAN: auto-issued with incorporation
  5. Udyam/MSME: FREE
  6. GST: ₹500-2,000 via CA (if applicable)
  7. DPIIT Startup Recognition: FREE
  8. Shops & Establishment: ₹500-2,000 (state-specific)
Total Cost: ₹15,000 - ₹35,000
Industry Notes: FSSAI for food (₹100-500 basic), IT startups need no extra license

--- LARGE BUDGET (₹10,00,000 - ₹1,00,00,000) - GROWTH STARTUP ---
All Medium requirements PLUS:
  - Trademark: ₹4,500 (individual) or ₹9,000 (company) per class
  - Professional CA/CS for ongoing compliance
  - EPF/ESI if >10/20 employees
  - Employment contracts + ESOP policy
  - Industry-specific licenses
Total Setup: ₹50,000 - ₹1,50,000

--- ENTERPRISE (Above ₹1,00,00,000) ---
All Large PLUS: Board structure, secretarial compliance, SEBI if raising public funds

--- 2026 KEY REGULATORY CHANGES ---
- Deep-tech startups: Classified as startups for up to 20 YEARS (2026 update)
- Revenue threshold for DPIIT benefits: ₹300 crore (raised from ₹100 crore)
- Tax holiday: Now covers startups incorporated before April 1, 2030
- Credit Guarantee: Cover raised from ₹10 crore to ₹20 crore (Budget 2025-26)
- Labour Codes: 4 codes to simplify and replace 44 labour laws (implementation ongoing)
- GST simplifications: Multiple rate rationalizations, simpler compliance for small businesses

--- INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC LICENSES ---
Food Business: FSSAI License — Basic (FREE), State (₹2,000-5,000), Central (₹7,500)
FinTech/Payment: RBI Payment Aggregator license (complex, ₹25L+ capital needed)
HealthTech: Telemedicine guidelines 2020; Clinical Establishment Act registration
Import-Export: IEC Code from DGFT website (₹500)
Online Education: No mandatory license required
Gaming (Real Money): TDS compliance mandatory; state gambling laws vary
Pharmacy/Drug: Drug license from state FDA required
NBFC: RBI registration (minimum ₹2 crore capital, lengthy process)
Travel Agency: IATA optional; state tourism license if running tours

=== FLAGSHIP NATIONAL SCHEMES ===

1. Startup India Initiative (2016, UPDATED 2026)
Eligibility: DPIIT-recognized startups — now up to 10 years old (20 years for deep-tech)

Tax Holiday: 3-year income tax exemption under Section 80-IAC

Self-certification: 6 labour laws + 3 environmental laws (saves compliance cost)
Fast-track patents: 80% rebate on patent fees + expedited examination
Apply FREE: startupindia.gov.in | Takes 2-3 working days

Corpus: ₹10,000 crore fresh NEW allocation
